previous projects

decoding k-pop: a computational analysis of musical elements
What makes K-Pop “K-Pop”? Are there fundamental stylistic differences between Korean Pop music and Western Pop music? If so, what musical elements, including key modulation, tonality, formal structures, and timbre, define K-Pop?
I programmed original key-finding algorithms using Python, R, and humdrumR to visualize key modulations and key profiles across large datasets. Also developed a classifier to predict K-Pop’s musical characteristics using a neural network while comparing various machine learning models and employing statistical tests such as regression, Akaike Information Criterion, and odds ratio analysis to identify key musical features that distinguish K-Pop from Western music.

beyond legal reformation: trans community mutual aid through tiktok
This project examines how young trans men TikTok content creators engage in community mutual aid through their TikTok content amid the influx of anti-trans legislation, as a window into how these anti-trans bills are directly and eminently impacting young people.
I developed a comprehensive coding system categorizing over 1000 TikTok videos of 20 transgender youth creators to assess how their content evolved and engaged in community mutual aid. In response to prevailing anti-trans narratives found within these laws, trans men responded to them in various ways including humor, “thirst traps,” or simply sharing their experiences with navigating their transness.
